## Errors and limits

- No misclassified samples in the final configuration.
- The corpus is small and was written alongside the rules, so these numbers are an optimistic upper bound; treat them as a regression test, not a benchmark.
- Static rules are pattern-based: novel phrasing of an injection, or logic split across files, can evade them. The LLM judge only reviews rule hits; it does not search for new issues.
